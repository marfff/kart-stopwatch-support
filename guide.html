How to use the stopwatch

A quick reference for four-driver manual kart timing.

1. Set up your drivers

Enter a name and kart number for any or all of the four independent stopwatches.

2. Start a driver

Press START as the driver crosses your chosen start/finish line. Other drivers can be started at different times.

3. Record laps

At each start/finish crossing, press LAP. The stopwatch stores the completed lap time and immediately begins the next lap. Times are displayed to hundredths of a second.

4. Record optional sector splits

For a three-sector lap, press SPLIT · S2 when Sector 2 starts, press SPLIT · S3 when Sector 3 starts, then press LAP at the finish line. That LAP press also completes the final sector. On other laps, you may press LAP only.

5. Interpret colours

Gold: overall fastest lap. Purple: driver's personal best. Green: improvement against the preceding lap or comparable split. Red: slower than the preceding lap or comparable split.

6. Review results

END TIMERS opens session reporting. The report ranks drivers by their fastest recorded lap and shows available splits and calculated theoretical laps. Unrecorded sectors are omitted or left blank.

7. Save a report

Tap Save Report to Photos. After iOS confirms saving, find the report in your Photos library. A new session can be started through the timer screen.

Manual timing depends on operator accuracy. Theoretical best laps use independently recorded fastest sectors and are indicative only.
