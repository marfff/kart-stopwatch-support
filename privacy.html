Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy describes the intended behaviour of Kart Multi Driver Stopwatch for iPhone and iPad.

Information you enter

Driver names, kart numbers, manually recorded lap times and sector splits are used to operate the app and generate session reports. Driver names may be personal data if they identify individuals.

Storage and sharing

Session information is designed to remain on your device. The app does not require an account or registration. When you choose to save a report, a copy is added to your Photos library. Any subsequent sharing or synchronisation of that image through your device or photo services is controlled by your settings and actions.

Photos permission

We request permission to add report images to your Photos library when you choose to save a report. This permission is not needed for ordinary stopwatch operation. You can review permissions in iOS Settings.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current app is intended to have no developer-run analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party sign-in. No user account is required. This statement must be checked against the version actually submitted to the App Store.

Data retention and deletion

Recorded sessions and locally stored app information can be managed through the app's session controls or removed by deleting the app, subject to iOS storage behaviour. Reports already saved in Photos are managed separately in Photos.

Children

The app is a stopwatch tool and does not provide a social network or child account system. Operators should use care when entering and sharing the names of young drivers.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will appear at this address.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy, use the contact details on the Support page.
