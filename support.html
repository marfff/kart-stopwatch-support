Common questions

How do I start a driver?

Enter a driver name and kart number if desired, then press START for that driver at the start/finish line. Each stopwatch starts independently.

How do I record a lap?

Press LAP whenever the driver crosses the start/finish line. The recorded lap finishes and the next lap begins automatically.

How do I record sectors?

Optionally press the SPLIT control at intermediate sector boundaries. For a standard three-sector lap, press SPLIT · S2, then SPLIT · S3; pressing LAP at the finish line completes Sector 3 and the lap. You may record lap-only and sector-recorded laps in one session.

How do I save a report?

End the session, open the report, and choose Save Report to Photos. On first use, allow permission to add photos. Check the Photos app for your report.

Are these official race times?

No. All times rely on the operator's button presses and are not official circuit timing results.
